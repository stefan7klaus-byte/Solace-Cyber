Never send seed phrases, private keys, passwords, or remote-access codes through a contact form, email, or chat.

Pause on pressure

Guaranteed recovery, urgent payment demands, unsolicited messages, and requests to install remote-access tools are warning signs.

Know the warning signs

How to interpret common recovery risks.

The checklist is an educational aid, not a technical scan of your device or wallet. It cannot verify a provider, inspect a wallet, or determine whether recovery is possible.

!

Requests for secret credentials

A seed phrase or private key can give direct control over assets. Never disclose these to a recovery provider, support agent, stranger, or website. Do not share one-time codes or remote-access credentials either.

⌛

Urgency and guaranteed results

Pressure to pay immediately, promises of guaranteed recovery, fabricated deadlines, or claims that a fee must be paid to “release” recovered funds are serious warning signs. Pause and verify independently.

↗

Unexpected links and software

Do not install remote-access tools or open files sent by unsolicited contacts. Navigate to official wallet or exchange support through a trusted bookmark or independently verified website.

▤

Preserve original evidence

Keep original devices and backups unchanged where practical. If files may be important, create a separate copy before experimenting and consider qualified assistance for damaged storage media.

✓

Verify terms in writing

Before paying or sending files, ask for a clear description of the work, costs, privacy practices, data retention, expected communication, and known limitations.

⛨

If you already shared a phrase

Assume the wallet may be compromised. Using a trusted device and official wallet guidance, consider moving remaining assets to a new wallet you control. Never reuse an exposed phrase, and be alert for follow-up recovery scams.

Keep your first message non-sensitive.

Wallet type, approximate date, device type, and a plain-language description of the issue are usually a safer starting point than screenshots or files containing private information.
