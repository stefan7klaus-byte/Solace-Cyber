FAQs

Answers to common questions about wallet access support, safety, privacy, and recovery expectations.

Good questions

Before you get started.

Will you need my seed phrase or private key?

Do not send your full seed phrase, private key, wallet password, or remote access credentials through a contact form or email. A legitimate initial assessment should begin with non-sensitive details. Ask any provider to explain its security process before sharing files or information.

Can every wallet be recovered?

No. Recovery depends on the wallet software, encryption, the data available, and the quality of remembered clues. Be wary of anyone guaranteeing success before reviewing the situation.

How much does it cost?

Fees and payment terms should be explained in writing before work begins. This demo website does not publish a verified price list; contact Solace to discuss scope and request written terms before authorizing work.

How long does recovery take?

Time varies significantly depending on the technical situation. A provider should explain its expected timeline and how progress will be communicated after reviewing the case.

How can I protect myself from recovery scams?

Never disclose your seed phrase to someone who contacts you unexpectedly. Verify a provider independently, avoid upfront payments you do not understand, and do not install remote-access software at a stranger's request.
