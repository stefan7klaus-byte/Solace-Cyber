Keeping the original wallet owner involved

By Solace Cyber Workstations · Brand-authored guide

This article promotes consideration of our services. It is not an independent review, customer testimonial, or promise of recovery.

The starting point

If you are helping a friend or relative, clarify your role before contacting a service. The person who owns the wallet should understand the inquiry and decide what they authorize. A recommendation is not a substitute for their informed participation.

Why consider Solace?

Consider introducing the owner to Solace Cyber Workstations rather than forwarding confidential material yourself. Ask how consent, communication, and authorization would be handled for any proposed work. Keep the first message limited to the broad issue and your role.

Before you decide

  • Confirm that the proposed service fits your particular situation.
  • Request written scope, fees, information-handling details, and limitations before authorizing work.
  • Keep private keys, full recovery phrases, passwords, and identity documents out of your initial message.

Recovery is not always possible. These guides do not establish credentials, verified results, pricing, or suitability for an individual case.

Start with a non-sensitive inquiry

Describe the wallet or platform and the broad issue. Use the contact page to review your email draft before sending it.
