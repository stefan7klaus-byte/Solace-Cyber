Before you contact us

Prepare useful details—without exposing secrets.

A short, organized summary can make an initial conversation more useful while protecting your wallet and personal information.

01

Identify the wallet

Note the wallet name, approximate year it was created, device or operating system used, and whether it was a software wallet, hardware wallet, or exchange account.

02

Describe the access problem

Explain what changed: a forgotten password, missing device, damaged drive, incomplete backup, or uncertainty about which wallet was used. Include only details you are comfortable sharing.

03

List available materials

Make a private inventory of old devices, backup locations, wallet files, receipts, and notes. Do not upload them until you understand the purpose, security arrangements, and written scope.

04

Record previous attempts

Keep a simple note of steps already tried and any error messages. Avoid modifying the original drive or repeatedly attempting guesses when the consequences are unclear.

A closer look at the process

From first questions to a clear, informed next step.

Solace Cyber Workstations helps people organize digital-asset access problems, understand possible options, and approach the next step carefully. The process begins with the facts available—not a promise that every wallet can be recovered.

01

Tell us what happened

Start with a plain-language description: which wallet or platform you used, approximately when you created it, what access problem occurred, and whether you still have the original device or backups. You do not need to share passwords or wallet secrets.

02

Organize the available evidence

Make a private inventory of relevant devices, wallet files, backup drives, software versions, transaction IDs, public wallet addresses, and any error messages. Keep originals safe and distinguish confirmed facts from guesses.

03

Identify the type of problem

Different situations need different approaches. A missing device, a damaged drive, an encrypted wallet file, a forgotten password, an incomplete recovery phrase, and an exchange-account issue are not the same problem and may have different limits.

04

Discuss realistic options

Review what can reasonably be checked, what information is missing, and which actions might put the original data at risk. Some cases may call for official platform support, a qualified data-recovery specialist, legal advice, or no further technical work.

05

Agree on scope before work

Before authorizing any paid work, request written details of the proposed tasks, permissions, fees, possible extra costs, privacy safeguards, storage and deletion practices, timeline expectations, and circumstances where work would stop.

06

Review findings and next steps

Ask for a clear explanation of what was checked, what was found, what remains uncertain, and what the next options are. Keep your own records and never feel pressured to continue if the explanation or terms are unclear.

Crypto recovery support

How we approach cryptocurrency access cases.

For Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other digital-asset wallets, the first step is to understand the wallet type and the source of the access problem. We may discuss the history of the wallet, the software or hardware involved, whether a backup exists, and whether public blockchain records can provide useful context.

Public transaction research can help explain activity associated with an address, but it cannot reveal a private key, establish real-world identity by itself, or reverse a completed blockchain transaction. A visible balance is not proof that wallet access can be restored.

Where old devices or files may be important, preserving the original data is a priority. Avoid experimenting on the only copy, installing unverified recovery tools, or repeatedly guessing credentials if you do not understand the risks.

✓
Wallet and device historyIdentify the wallet software or hardware, approximate dates, operating system, and what has changed since access was last available.
✓
Backup and file review planningList possible wallet files and backups privately; confirm the purpose and secure handling before sharing any sensitive file.
✓
Public address researchUse public transaction records for context only, without confusing blockchain visibility with control over the assets.
✓
Security-first next stepsKeep seed phrases, private keys, passwords, and authentication codes secret at all times.
